# Dairy Feeding Management System (DFMS)

## Vision

Build a complete ERP platform for Dairy Farm Feeding Department.

The application manages the entire feeding operation from raw material receiving until feeding completion while integrating with Google Spreadsheet.

---

# Goals

Reduce manual work.

Increase feeding accuracy.

Improve monitoring.

Provide real-time information.

Support management decisions.

---

# Business Rules

Loader cannot exceed target weight without confirmation.

Mixing duration should be monitored.

Distribution starts only after Mixing finishes.

Warehouse stock decreases automatically.

Reports generated automatically.

All activities stored in history.
